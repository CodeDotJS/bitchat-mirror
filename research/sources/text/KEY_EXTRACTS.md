## S06 — KeepItOn 2024

- India: **84** shutdowns — most in any democracy.
- People in **16** states/UTs affected.
- Top offenders inside India included Manipur (21), Haryana (12), J&K (12).
- Of 84: **41** protest-related; **23** communal violence-related (per report narrative).

## S07 / S08 — J&K 552 days

- Communications restrictions from Aug 2019; full 4G restored Feb 2021.
- Characterised as among the longest internet / communications restrictions in a democracy.
- SFLC phrasing: regained 4G after **552 days** of no-to-low internet.

## S12 — Anuradha Bhasin (careful holding)

- Speech & expression via internet → Art. 19(1)(a).
- Trade/business via internet → Art. 19(1)(g).
- Restrictions: reasonableness + proportionality.
- Indefinite suspension under Suspension Rules: not permitted.
- Court **declined** to declare free-standing right to internet access.

## S13–S15 — Sahyog

- Karnataka HC 2025: upholds Sahyog; rejects X “censorship portal” challenge.
