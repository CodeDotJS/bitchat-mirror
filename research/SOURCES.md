## Notes on methodology differences

- **Access Now #KeepItOn** counts deliberate, intentional shutdowns with human-rights framing; annual country totals are the gold standard for “how often.”
- **SFLC.in** is India-specific and often better for *duration* and state-level order trails.
- **Top10VPN / NetBlocks COST** estimate economic loss for *major* restrictions; they undercount tiny village-level cuts — do not mix with Access Now counts as if identical.
- **Freedom House FOTN** is a composite 0–100 score (obstacles / content limits / user-rights violations), not a shutdown counter.
