Why this exists

We do not believe in internet censorship

Communication tools should not disappear because a government or platform decides they are inconvenient. bitchat is built for peer-to-peer mesh over Bluetooth — so people can still message nearby when the wider internet is filtered, throttled, or switched off. This site exists to keep the Android builds findable when app stores and GitHub links get hard to reach.

Internet freedom

Partly Free

Freedom House’s Freedom on the Net 2024 rates India “Partly Free” (score 50/100).

Shutdowns, 2016–2023

771+

Access Now reports India imposed more internet shutdowns than the rest of the world combined in that period.

Longest blackout

552 days

Jammu & Kashmir’s 2019–2021 communications restrictions included the world’s longest recorded internet shutdown.

Takedown pressure

Rising fast

Blocking requests to platforms roughly doubled from ~12,600 in 2024 to ~24,300 in 2025 after the Sahyog portal automated government takedowns.

India has blocked websites and apps under the IT Act, ordered ISPs to filter URLs, and repeatedly suspended mobile data during protests, exams, and unrest — including months-long blackouts in places like Manipur. Official reasons vary; the effect is the same: people lose the ability to speak, organise, learn, and stay informed.

Centralised app stores and cloud chat make that easy. A BLE mesh does not. When the cell tower is dark, nearby phones can still pass messages hop by hop. Mirroring APKs is a small act of infrastructure: if one download link dies, another copy remains.

Context drawn from Internet censorship in India (Wikipedia) and the sources cited there, including Freedom House and Access Now. This mirror is unofficial and not affiliated with bitchat’s upstream maintainers.

Install & verify

Sideload

  1. Allow your browser to install apps: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.
  2. Install the APK, then open bitchat.
  3. Grant Nearby devices / Bluetooth. Location may be requested — that is an Android BLE scanning rule, not GPS tracking by the app.

Verify

  1. Copy the SHA-256 shown next to the file (or open checksums).
  2. Run sha256sum your.apk and compare.
  3. Source for each build is the linked upstream commit. bitchat is GPLv3 — you can inspect and rebuild it.
